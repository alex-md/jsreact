How to play

The secret is a whole number in the displayed range. Every clue describes it. Combine them until only one number fits, then check your answer.

Clue count sets the length; difficulty sets the mathematics. Hard and Extreme use distinct clue types. Small groups of clues must leave a wider field of candidates to work through. More clues do not automatically mean an easier puzzle.

Every published puzzle is checked across its entire range. Any subset missing a clue still has multiple possible answers.
