How to read your appraisal

Auction: compare with a competitive auction sale. Marketplace: compare with a direct listing. Brokerage: compare with a broker-assisted sale.

Use recent comparable sales, the name’s length, extension, and buyer demand alongside these estimates. An appraisal does not check availability or trademark rights.
